Buy List Explorer

A buy list is every card a model scores at or above a threshold. Cards on the list that sat unsold park cash; cards left off that went on to sell are missed sellers. Drag the threshold and watch two lists fill side by side on synthetic cards: a full logistic model and a logistic on trailing sales only (velocity), which any buyer can see for free.
